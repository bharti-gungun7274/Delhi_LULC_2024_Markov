DELHI LULC 2024 CLASS CODEBOOK

Raster: Delhi_LULC_2024_FINAL_NODATA.tif
Resolution: 10 m
CRS: EPSG:32643

CLASS VALUES:

0 = Water
1 = Trees
2 = Grass
3 = Flooded vegetation
4 = Crops
5 = Shrub & scrub
6 = Built-up
7 = Bare ground
8 = Snow & ice
255 = NoData / outside study area

IMPORTANT:
Values 0–8 are LULC classes.
255 is NoData and should not be treated as a LULC class.
